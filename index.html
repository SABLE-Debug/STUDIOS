PART 03

From idea to animated 3D asset.

The simplified flow. Higgsfield for cinematic video. CSS 3D and Three.js for interactive moments in code. Zero new tools to learn outside the locked stack. From idea to deployed asset: 5 hours of focused work.

Step 01 · 30 min
Concept the asset
One sentence describing what it is and what it does. Vague concepts produce vague assets. "The Meridian mark rotates in 3D space and the camera orbits it on scroll." That's a usable concept.
Tool · Pen and paper
Step 02 · 1 hour
Reference library
Pull 5–10 video clips of the exact motion you want. Twitter, Vimeo, Awwwards, Bruno Simon, Lusion, Active Theory. Save to a folder. You're building a vocabulary your future self can point at.
Tool · Save folder
Step 03 · 1 hour
Generate base imagery in Higgsfield
Concept the asset visually. The Meridian mark, abstract architectural forms, dark spaces with gold light. Higgsfield generates the still frames you'll animate from.
Tool · Higgsfield · in stack
Step 04 · 30 min
Cinematic camera moves
Drop the still into Higgsfield's image-to-video. Pick a camera move — orbit, dolly push, slow rotate. Generate at 4K. Export as muted autoplay loop. This is the hero video.
Same tool · same session
Step 05 · 2 hours
Interactive 3D in code
For things that need to react to scroll or cursor — CSS 3D transforms for the lighter moments, Three.js for the heavy ones. The Meridian mark in this playbook is pure CSS 3D, no library. Free, fast, native.
Tool · VS Code · in stack
Step 06 · 30 min
Compose in HTML
Drop the Higgsfield video into a `<video autoplay muted loop>` tag. Drop the CSS 3D mark inline. Layer typography on top with absolute positioning. Single HTML file, GitHub Pages-ready.
Output · single file
Step 07 · 30 min
Ship
Push to GitHub. GitHub Pages serves it. The asset is live on the production AKHARA site. From idea to deployed 3D asset, 5 hours of work.

PART 07

The lifestyle at the goal.

$50K MRR with 82% gross margin nets roughly $32K/month after software, taxes, and reinvestment. That number doesn't tie you to South Africa, the US, or anywhere else.

Realistic year-one base

Cape Town, Lisbon, Mexico City, Bali, Bangkok. Living costs under $3K/month in any of them. Housing in the top 10% of the local market. Time zone flexible because every client interaction is async-first by design.

Year two and beyond

Once 5 whales lock and one case study is public, location is purely preference. Move every quarter if it suits. The agency runs on the same Notion brain regardless of your postcode.

The Closer runs 18 hours a day. The Operator runs 24/7. Your hours are spent reviewing, deciding, and showing up to discovery calls. Not grinding manual outreach.

Months 0–3

3–5 focused hours on free days. Less on flying or school days. Output is condensed — most of the work you'd do manually is already done by The Closer overnight. You wake up to triage, approvals, and 2–3 booked discovery calls.

Months 3–6

2–4 hours on free days. The Architect runs onboarding for new clients. The Operator handles every existing client. Your time goes to discovery calls (booked by The Closer), quality control, and pricing decisions.

Months 6–12

1–3 hours on free days. The agency runs whether you log in or not. You spend time on strategy, whale-level prospect calls, and brand. Most days, the work is choosing what not to do.

Year two ceiling

Solo, sustainably, the cap is around $1.2M ARR. Past that, quality fractures unless you hire. The first hire is an ops lead at $60K/year. The second is a junior strategist. From there it's a real agency, not a solo flex.

The brand. The system prompts (proprietary IP). The case studies. The Notion brain. The buyer intelligence file. The AKHARA Wordmark and Meridian Mark. Trademarked at month 9.

What you don't own

Claude, Make, Slack, Notion. You rent the infrastructure. Your moat isn't the tools — it's the brand, the prompt library, the buyer language packs, the proven results, and the operator.

Exit optionality

At $1M ARR with strong retention, AKHARA is sellable to a private equity rollup at 4–6x EBITDA. That's a $3–5M exit on a 18–30 month build. Or it's never sold and it compounds. Both options are real.

Not money. Focus, taste, and the willingness to keep showing up to discovery calls until your close rate sharpens. The first 90 days are condensed, not endless — most of the work runs while you sleep.

Cost
  • Friendships that get loose maintenance in M0–3
  • Late nights on weeks 4 and 12 specifically
  • The constant temptation to discount, to hand-hold, to over-explain
Reward

By month 12: an asset that generates $50K/month while you sleep. By month 24: a sellable business or a compounding income stream. By 30: optional retirement on $5M+ if it scales.

Three things kill agencies at this stage:

01 · Discounting

The 10% annual prepay is the only allowed discount. Every other "just this once" cut signals to the universe that the price isn't real. The brand dies the day you cave.

02 · Bad clients

One nightmare client at month 3 eats 40% of your hours and produces 0% of your revenue growth. Fire them. The Closer screens for fit before discovery — use it ruthlessly.

03 · Drift

Building features instead of selling. Iterating the site instead of cold-calling. The product is finished enough at v1. The next 9 months are sales, sales, sales.
